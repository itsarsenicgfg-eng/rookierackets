# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents of youth (ages 5–18) in Cary, NC and the broader Triangle area. They're looking for free, structured activities for their kids. Secondary: youth who discover the site through school or word of mouth. Tertiary: partner organizations (schools, nonprofits, community centers) evaluating whether to host a session.

## Product Purpose

Rookie Rackets is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit running free badminton workshops for youth in Cary, NC. Founded by Green Hope High School student Adithya Burisetty. The site informs parents about upcoming events, builds a waitlist for session announcements, documents the organization's history and credibility, and establishes trust through transparency about the free model and the quality of coaching.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, accessible, community-first. A student started this — the warmth and genuine origin should come through, balanced against the credibility of US National Coach-level training. Not corporate. Not sterile. Not a theme park. Confident without being polished-cold.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (purple gradient heroes, hero-metric stat grids, card grids with icons)
- Corporate nonprofit sites (distant, donation-forward, boardroom aesthetic)
- Youth sports camp sites that feel overdesigned or chaotic (loud, busy, theme-park energy)

## Design Principles

1. Credibility through specificity — exact names, dates, and participant counts beat vague superlatives
2. Accessible by design — free is the product; the site reinforces that no barrier exists to participate
3. Community before brand — the kids and families are the story, not the founders
4. Restrained energy — the sport is fast; the site is focused and clear

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum throughout. Site serves youth and families of all backgrounds and abilities. Reduced motion support required on all animations.
